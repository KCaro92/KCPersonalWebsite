My Hobbies

When I am not at school or working my two jobs, I like to destress by being creative. On average, I enjoy drawing, painting, baking, and crocheting. I've been crocheting for years, and it’s the hobby I turn to the most when I need to relax. I love creating things because it gives me a sense of accomplishment, especially since crocheting is a time‑consuming hobby. I usually make amigurumi dolls, but sometimes I make pillows or bags. Recently, I’ve been doing tapestry crochet, which involves switching colors between different skeins to create a design as you work. It’s always fun to learn new techniques throughout my crochet journey.
